## About

**SOJKA.PRO** is a private R&D organization focused on turning observable data into useful intelligence and better decisions.

We explore and develop practical systems at the intersection of:

- artificial intelligence;
- data and semantic analysis;
- automation and agentic workflows;
- software engineering;
- research and decision support;
- reusable digital infrastructure.

We prefer small, verifiable systems over complexity for its own sake.

> **Observe reality. Understand it. Act with judgment.**

### CORE

The private SOJKA.PRO control and governance layer.

CORE maintains organization authority, current state, foundations, project lifecycle and shared operating boundaries.

### MarAI 🐝

The primary AI representative/operator and R&D identity of SOJKA.PRO.

MarAI is model- and provider-agnostic. Her role is to turn Owner intent into useful, verifiable progress while preserving evidence, context boundaries and human authority.

### 𝔐𝔞𝔤𝔫𝔲𝔰 Workshop

A separate Owner-led business workshop focused on discovering and validating real online opportunities.

It is intentionally independent from the SOJKA.PRO business canon, while remaining visible to CORE and MarAI as part of the wider Owner workspace.

## Public / private boundary

This GitHub organization profile is the **public presentation layer** of SOJKA.PRO.

Most active R&D, internal governance, MarAI development and experimental work are private by design.

Public repositories, releases and research outputs appear here only when they are ready to stand on their own.

```text
public profile ≠ internal state
presentation ≠ authority
capability ≠ permission
```
