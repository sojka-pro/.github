# SOJKA.PRO

**Original media brands, worlds & digital products.**

SOJKA.PRO is an independent, owner-led creative studio building original social media brands, fictional worlds and digital experiences with AI-assisted production and human editorial direction.

We develop distinctive identities, recurring stories, characters and products designed to earn attention, invite participation and give people a reason to return.

## Worlds in development

- **YAYBITS** — Little bits of joy. Warm, playful micro-stories and everyday moments.
- **CrownVerse** — An original fictional universe, developed through characters, lore and future interactive experiences.

Each world has its own voice and visual identity. Projects in development are not claims of a live service or published release.

## How we build

Original ideas. Consistent identities. Careful production. Measured learning.

Our internal SOC Engine connects research, brand design, content production, distribution and the development of our own products. We use AI as a creative and technical tool; editorial judgment stays with the creator.
